ShieldUPI Architecture & NPCI Integration Blueprint

Designed for frictionless integration into existing Indian payment applications (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) and bank core systems. The engine operates strictly within the mandatory <120ms NPCI SLA window.

Lightweight Android/iOS SDK hooks inspecting accessibility permissions, active MediaProjection screen casting, paste buffers, and telephony call state without capturing sensitive PII.
